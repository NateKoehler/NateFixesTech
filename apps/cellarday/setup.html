Set up your club sheet

Sign in with the Google account that keeps your club members. This creates one Google Sheet and one folder for pickup signatures in that account’s Drive. Cellarday can only see the files it creates, never the rest of your Drive.

Next steps

  1. Open the sheet and go to the Members tab.
  2. Paste your member list under the headers, keeping the same column order. Leave Member ID blank; the iPad fills it in.
  3. Pick each member’s Preference (Mixed, Red only, White only).
  4. Enter releases in the Releases tab, one row per wine, and set Ready to Y when members have been charged.
