What it does

🩺

Health check

See storage, drive health, memory, Windows Update, antivirus and your internet connection on one screen, with a clear explanation of each result. No scare tactics.

🔧

One-click fixes

Fix websites that won't load, a frozen taskbar, stuck printing, a wrong clock and more. Every fix tells you what it does before it runs.

🧭

Home tech helper

Step-by-step guides that ask one simple question at a time: Wi‑Fi not working, printer setup and problems, storage full, and what to do if you think you got scammed.

🛡️

Scam checker

Paste a text or email you're unsure about. The app points out common scam tricks like gift card requests, fake toll texts and look-alike links. Your message never leaves your PC.

✉️

Help from a real person

Still stuck? Send Nate a request in a couple of clicks, with a summary of what you already tried, so you don't have to explain it twice.
