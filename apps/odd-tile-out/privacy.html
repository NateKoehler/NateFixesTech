Privacy Policy

Odd Tile Out: Color Game · Effective September 24, 2026

Odd Tile Out is published by NateFixesTech LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we don't collect any.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else, and it works fully offline.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best score for each game track on your device so it can show your personal bests. This stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted if you delete the app.

Sharing your results

If you tap "Share my results", the app opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short text summary of your scores. Nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it, and we never see it.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Not a medical tool

Odd Tile Out is a game. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose any condition. Your scores are not health information and are not collected.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before that change reaches the app.
