Cellarday Privacy Policy

For Cellarday, the iPad app wine bars and shops use to track wine club pickups.

Effective October 17, 2026

The short version

  • Cellarday keeps everything in the venue's own Google Sheet and Google Drive. NateFixesTech has no servers and never receives the venue's data.
  • The app can only see the files it creates. It can't see the rest of the venue's Google Drive.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. Nothing is sold or shared.
  • Cellarday never emails, texts or calls club members, and never handles payments.

Who we are

Cellarday is made by NateFixesTech LLC, a computer and phone repair business in San Benito County, California.

Questions about this policy: support@natefixestech.com

Signing in with Google

Staff sign in with the venue's Google account. Cellarday asks Google for only these permissions:

  • Your email address, to show which account is signed in.
  • See, edit, create and delete only the Google Drive files Cellarday uses (Google calls this "drive.file"). This lets the app create one Cellarday sheet and one signatures folder, and work with those. It can't open, list or change any other file in the account.

The sign-in is kept in the iPad's secure storage (the iOS Keychain) so staff don't have to sign in every time.

What Cellarday stores, and where

Everything is saved in the venue's own Google account:

  • The Cellarday sheet: the club members the venue enters (names, club, contact details and notes), each club's releases and wines, who has paid for each release, pickups (date, which wines, who picked up) and staff notes.
  • The signatures folder: an image of each member's signature when they pick up.

On the iPad, Cellarday keeps a copy of changes that haven't been saved to Google yet, so nothing is lost when the internet drops. They're sent as soon as the iPad is back online. Deleting the app removes them from the iPad.

Who can see the data

  • The venue, and anyone the venue chooses to share its Google Sheet with.
  • NateFixesTech only sees a venue's sheet if the venue shares it with us to get help, and only for that purpose.
  • Google stores the files under its own privacy policy.

We never sell, rent or share the data with anyone, except if the law requires it.

Google user data

Cellarday's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from Google is used only to run the app's features for the venue, is never used for advertising, and is never transferred to anyone else.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Stop the app's access: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions in the venue's Google account and remove Cellarday.
  • Delete the data: delete the Cellarday sheet and the Cellarday signatures folder from Google Drive.
  • Remove it from the iPad: delete the Cellarday app.

Club members who want their information changed or removed can ask the venue, which controls the sheet.

Payments

Cellarday doesn't take or store payments or card details. Venues handle payments in their own payment system, such as Square.

Children

Cellarday is a business tool for venue staff and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
