Odd Tile Out: Color Game for iPhone and iPad

Need help or found a bug?
Email support@natefixestech.com. Include your device model and iOS version if you can. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

Pick one or more tracks, then tap the 3×3 patch of dots that doesn't match the rest. Each time you find it, it moves and gets harder to see. Three wrong taps end the track.

Why did I score differently on another device?

Screens vary in color and brightness. For the most consistent results, turn brightness up, switch off Night Shift, True Tone and any blue-light filters, and hold the device at arm's length.

Is this a color blindness test?

No. Odd Tile Out is a game, not a diagnosis. If a result surprises you, an optometrist can run a proper color vision exam.

How do I reset my best scores?

Best scores are stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears them.

Privacy

The app collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
